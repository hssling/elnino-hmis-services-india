# Table 1. Indicator crosswalk and harmonised definitions

| Indicator | Role | HMIS items, FY2012-13 to 2016-17 | HMIS items, FY2017-18 to 2020-21 | Harmonised definition | National April/March 2017 ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ANC registration | Primary service (H7, H9) | 1.1 | 1.1 | Pregnant women registered for antenatal care; the same item in every format. | 0.920 |

Items are the HMIS data-item numbers of the monthly state reports; "+" means the items are added. Each item was matched by its text in every financial year, and the full crosswalk, with the item texts and the file each was verified in, is in the supplement. The April/March 2017 ratio is the national ratio of the pre-gate state values for the units reporting both months, around the change of the HMIS reporting format in April 2017; it is descriptive, and every model contains a format-change term.
